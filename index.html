Virtual chips are used exclusively for entertainment within BLACKJACK 888.

Virtual chips have no monetary value and cannot be withdrawn, exchanged for cash, transferred for monetary compensation, or redeemed for real-world prizes.

Locally stored chip balances and gameplay progress may be lost if the application is uninstalled or its data is cleared.
