Privacy Policy

Status: Draft — pending final privacy review

BLACKJACK 888 is a simulated blackjack game intended for adults aged 18 and older. Virtual chips cannot be redeemed for real money or prizes.

1. Information We Collect

BLACKJACK 888 does not require players to create an account or provide a name or email address to play.

Game information, such as virtual chip balances and preferences, may be stored locally on the device. Third-party services may collect additional information as described below.

2. Advertising

BLACKJACK 888 integrates Google AdMob to provide advertisements, including optional rewarded ads.

Google and its advertising partners may process advertising identifiers, device information, app interactions, and diagnostic information for purposes such as advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention.

Advertising choices and consent mechanisms will be provided where required by applicable law.

3. Virtual Chips

Virtual chips are used exclusively for entertainment within BLACKJACK 888. They have no monetary value and cannot be withdrawn, exchanged for cash, or redeemed for real-world prizes.

Locally stored chip balances may be lost if the application is uninstalled or its data is cleared.

4. Optional Purchases

Optional virtual-chip purchases may be introduced in future versions. If implemented, payments will be processed through Google Play Billing.

BLACKJACK 888 will not directly collect or store complete payment card details.

5. Customer Support

If you contact customer support, we may receive your email address and the information you voluntarily include in your message.

This information is used to respond to requests, investigate reported issues, and improve the game.

6. Children's Privacy

BLACKJACK 888 is intended for adults aged 18 and older and is not directed toward children.

7. Data Storage and Security

Certain gameplay information is stored locally. We take reasonable measures to protect information under our control, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Third-Party Services

BLACKJACK 888 uses or may use Google services, including AdMob and Google Play services. These services have their own privacy practices.

For additional information, review Google's Privacy Policy .

9. Your Privacy Choices

Players may clear locally stored gameplay information through their device's application settings. Clearing app data may permanently remove virtual chips and saved progress.

Players may request information about or deletion of personal information submitted through customer support, subject to applicable requirements.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the application evolves. Changes will be reflected by an updated effective date when the policy is finalized or revised.
